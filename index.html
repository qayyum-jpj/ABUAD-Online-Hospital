About Us

At ABUAD Online Hospital, We believe healthcare should be as close as your fingertips. Our platform is designed to connect patients with licensed doctors, making consultation, prescriptions,and health records accessible anytime, Anywhere.

With a focus on Innovation and care, We Combine The Expertise of Afe Babalola University's medical professionals with modern digital digital technology to provide a safe, fast, and reliable healthcare experience.

Whether You need an Urgent Consultation, routine check-up, Or access to your medical history, ABUAD Online Hospital Is your trusted health partner - ensuring that your health remains our top priority
